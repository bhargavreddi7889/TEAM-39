Administrative Circular Guidelines

1. Circular Issuance
1.1 All official announcements must be issued through authorized administrative circulars.
1.2 Circulars may pertain to examinations, holidays, placements, or academic activities.

2. Circular Format
2.1 Circulars must contain a title, subject, body, and issuing authority.
2.2 Circulars may be generated digitally and distributed electronically.

3. Validity
3.1 Circulars issued by authorized administrators are binding on students and faculty.
