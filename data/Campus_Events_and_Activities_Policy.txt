Campus Events and Activities Policy

1. Participation Eligibility
1.1 All registered students are eligible to participate in cultural and technical events.
1.2 Participation must not interfere with academic responsibilities.

2. Attendance Adjustment
2.1 Attendance may be adjusted for students officially representing the institute.
2.2 Prior approval must be obtained from the department.

3. Code of Conduct
3.1 Students must adhere to institutional code of conduct during events.
