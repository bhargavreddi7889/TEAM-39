B.Tech Examination Rules and Assessment Structure

1. Internal Assessment
1.1 Two mid-term examinations will be conducted: Mid-1 and Mid-2.
1.2 Each mid-term examination carries 20 marks.

2. End-Semester Examination
2.1 The end-semester examination carries 60 marks.
2.2 Students must satisfy attendance requirements to be eligible.

3. Examination Eligibility
3.1 Students detained due to attendance shortage are not eligible to write any examination.
3.2 Students with approved condonation may appear for examinations.

4. Malpractice
4.1 Students involved in examination malpractice will be subject to disciplinary action.
