ATTENDANCE POLICY – B.TECH PROGRAMS

1. Attendance is calculated subject-wise for each semester.
2. A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory to appear for End Semester Examinations.
3. Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation with valid medical or official proof.
4. Attendance below 65% is considered a shortage and the student will be detained for the semester.
5. Attendance is recorded for all theory, laboratory, and tutorial sessions.
6. Late entry beyond 10 minutes from the scheduled class start time will be marked absent.
7. Attendance records once finalized cannot be modified without HOD approval.
8. Students are responsible for regularly monitoring their attendance through the campus portal.
