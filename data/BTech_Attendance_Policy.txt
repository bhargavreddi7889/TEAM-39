Attendance Policy – B.Tech Programs

1. Minimum Attendance Requirement
1.1 Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject.

2. Attendance Shortage
2.1 Students with attendance below 75% are not eligible to appear for examinations.
2.2 Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for attendance condonation.
2.3 Attendance below 65% is not eligible for condonation under any circumstances.

3. Attendance Review
3.1 Attendance will be reviewed before Mid-Term examinations.
3.2 Attendance will be reviewed again before End-Semester examinations.

4. Condonation Rules
4.1 Condonation requests must be submitted with valid documentation.
4.2 Approval of condonation is at the discretion of the academic committee.
