Eligibility and Promotion Rules – B.Tech

1. Promotion Criteria
1.1 Students must secure a minimum of 40% marks in each subject.
1.2 Students must pass all mandatory subjects to be promoted to the next semester.

2. Detention Rules
2.1 Students failing to meet attendance or examination eligibility criteria will be detained.
2.2 Detained students are not eligible for placements or examinations.

3. Re-Registration
3.1 Detained students must re-register for the semester as per academic guidelines.
