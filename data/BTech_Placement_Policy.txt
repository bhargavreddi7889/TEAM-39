B.Tech Placement Policy – Academic Year 2024–25

1. General Eligibility Criteria
1.1 Students must have a minimum CGPA of 7.0 calculated up to the current semester.
1.2 Students must not have any active backlogs at the time of placement registration.
1.3 A minimum overall attendance of 75% is mandatory for placement eligibility.

2. Placement Registration
2.1 Eligible students must register through the official placement portal.
2.2 Registration deadlines will be communicated through official circulars.
2.3 Late registrations will not be accepted under any circumstances.

3. Company-Specific Eligibility
3.1 Some companies may specify higher CGPA cutoffs than the institute minimum.
3.2 Certain companies may restrict eligibility to specific branches or skill sets.
3.3 Company eligibility criteria supersede general placement rules.

4. Offer Acceptance Rules
4.1 Once a student is selected and an offer is made, the student is required to accept the offer.
4.2 Multiple offer policies will be governed by the institute placement guidelines.

5. Disqualification
5.1 Students found violating placement rules or misrepresenting information will be disqualified.
